# Visual Identity — Working System

## Status
This is a provisional direction for the first prototype. Final account name, logo, and exact brand expression remain open for owner approval.

## Art direction
Modern editorial explainer: a warm off-white canvas, deep ink typography, lime and amber accents, simple diagrams, restrained geometric forms, and occasional mascot appearances.

## Color tokens
| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Ink | `#17212B` | Headlines, outlines, key text |
| Background | Paper | `#F7F4EC` | Main canvas |
| Surface | White | `#FFFDF8` | Cards and panels |
| Secondary text | Muted | `#65717B` | Explanatory copy |
| Neutral | Soft | `#E8E4DA` | Secondary shapes |
| Accent | Lime | `#B7E36B` | Emphasis and highlights |
| Accent | Amber | `#F4B64A` | Warm highlights and mascot clothing |

## Typography
Use a strong sans-serif display face for headlines and a highly readable sans-serif for body copy. The first Remotion prototype uses Arial/Helvetica system fallbacks to avoid font-loading surprises. Replace these only after a font is selected and its licensing is checked.

## Composition principles
- 9:16 vertical canvas, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 40–60 seconds per standard video (50-second default).
- Keep headline blocks short and high-contrast.
- Use ample negative space and consistent outer margins.
- Keep important text away from platform UI areas.
- Animate with clear entrances and exits; avoid motion for motion's sake.
- Use consistent corner radius, outline weight, and spacing.

## Mascot usage
- The mascot is a supporting guide, not the whole identity.
- Use on hooks, transitions, explanatory gestures, or closing moments.
- Keep character colors and proportions consistent.
- Provided images are generated character references; crop files are not a production rig and should not be sliced into arbitrary moving parts.
