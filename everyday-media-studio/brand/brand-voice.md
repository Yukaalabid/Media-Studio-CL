# Brand Voice

## Voice traits
- Curious, not self-important.
- Clear, not simplistic.
- Warm, not overly familiar.
- Evidence-aware, not overconfident.
- Conversational, not full of forced slang.

## Writing rules
- Open with a scene, question, or familiar tension.
- Use short sentences and concrete examples.
- Explain one main thought at a time.
- Avoid inflated phrases such as "rahasia yang tidak pernah diketahui" unless literally true.
- Do not use "semua orang" when evidence only supports "sebagian orang" or "banyak orang".
- Close with an insight or genuine question, not a forced engagement trick.

## Hook formula examples
- "Pernah [situasi yang familiar]?"
- "Awalnya cuma mau [aksi kecil], lalu [hasil yang tidak disadari]."
- "Kenapa [fenomena sehari-hari] bisa terjadi?"
- "Kelihatannya sepele, tetapi [konsekuensi/pertanyaan]."
