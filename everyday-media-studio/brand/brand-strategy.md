# Brand Strategy — Working Draft

## Strategic direction
Build a broad-interest digital media account that turns familiar everyday experiences into clear, interesting explanations.

## Working brand promise
**Hal biasa, perspektif baru.**

This is a working line, not an approved final tagline.

## Positioning statement
For digitally active people who are curious about the world around them, this media account explains everyday habits, digital life, consumer behavior, productivity, and social phenomena through well-researched motion graphic stories lasting 40–60 seconds, with a 50-second default.

## Audience
- Primary: young adults and students who regularly consume short-form video.
- Secondary: curious general audiences who enjoy accessible explanations.

## Differentiation
- Start from relatable situations rather than abstract definitions.
- Explain one useful idea per video.
- Blend editorial storytelling with clean motion graphics.
- Keep a consistent voice and visual system across varied topics.

## Editorial boundaries
- Avoid becoming a random-facts aggregator.
- Avoid health/psychology diagnosis and unsupported pop-science claims.
- Avoid partisan rage-bait, humiliating humor, and misinformation.
- Avoid sales-first content during the first brand-building month.

## Success signals for month one
- Consistent publishing and visual quality.
- Strong early retention relative to the account's own baseline.
- Comments that show recognition, curiosity, or thoughtful discussion.
- Identification of 2–3 pillars and formats worth developing further.

## Video duration standard
All standard short-form videos must run 40–60 seconds. Plan around 50 seconds by default. A shorter or longer format requires explicit owner approval. Prioritize a complete, accurate explanation over filler.
