# Content Pillars

| Pillar | Coverage | Example question |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday Life | Daily routines, habits, familiar moments | Why do we open the fridge when we are not hungry? |
| Digital Life & Technology | Phones, apps, AI, internet, social platforms | Why do notifications pull our attention? |
| Money & Consumer Behavior | Shopping, spending, prices, promotions | Why does free shipping influence a purchase? |
| Productivity & Personal Growth | Study, work, time, focus, routines | Why is starting a task often harder than continuing it? |
| Social & Communication | Conversation, social patterns, culture | Why can a group chat suddenly go quiet? |

## Editorial test
Before approving a topic, check:
1. Relatable: does it start from a recognizable situation?
2. Curious: does it raise a real question?
3. Credible: can the explanation be supported?
4. Cohesive: does it fit the brand promise?
5. Visual: can it be explained clearly through motion graphics?
