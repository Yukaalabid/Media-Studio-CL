## Duration and pacing
Each calendar topic should be developed into a complete 40–60 second story. A useful starting split for a 50-second video is: hook 0–6s, situation 6–14s, explanation 14–34s, insight 34–44s, and engagement 44–50s. Adjust scene boundaries when the story needs it, while keeping the total within 40–60 seconds.

## Weekly review
After every 7 days, review: average watch time, retention curve if available, completion rate, comments, saves, shares, and production time. Compare each post against the account's own baseline. Do not select a winning pillar based on views alone.

## Research caution
Titles are editorial hypotheses, not proven scientific claims. Research each explanation before writing a script. Reframe or replace a topic if evidence is weak.
